## Brand & Style

This design system is built for high-conversion e-commerce environments where clarity, speed, and trust are paramount. The brand personality is dependable, efficient, and customer-centric, evoking a sense of absolute reliability and effortless transactions. 

We adopt a **Corporate / Modern** design style, leaning heavily on rigorous grid structures, high-contrast typography, and uncompromising functional utility. Every visual element is optimized to reduce cognitive load, guide the user's eye effortlessly toward product value, and streamline the path to purchase.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for high-conversion retail, balancing authoritative dark structural elements with vibrant, action-oriented accents. 

- **Primary (`#FF9900`):** The definitive conversion color. Reserved exclusively for primary calls-to-action, active states, and critical price highlights.
- **Secondary (`#232F3E`):** Used for navigation headers, structural containers, and high-emphasis enterprise framing.
- **Nav Dark (`#131A22`):** Deep charcoal-navy anchoring top-level global navigation and footer zones.
- **Neutral / Surface (`#FFFFFF` & `#EAEDED`):** Pure white content cards sit atop light cool-gray backgrounds to establish clear visual grouping.
- **Alert / Accent (`#CC0C39`):** Applied strictly to promotional badges, savings callouts, and error states.

## Typography

Typography establishes an uncompromising hierarchy designed for dense data scannability. We utilize **Inter** as a systematic, highly legible sans-serif that performs exceptionally across all viewports. 

Prices must command immediate attention: use `price-display` paired with superscript fractional units to optimize scanning velocity. Body text remains dark (`#0F1111`) on light backgrounds to maximize contrast and reduce eye strain during extended browsing sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a **Fluid grid** model structured around a 12-column layout on desktop devices, scaling down to 4 columns on mobile. 

Margins and gutters adapt responsively: desktop interfaces maintain generous 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margins with 16px (`1rem`) gutters between product cards. Spacing tokens must be applied strictly to layout dimensions and padding, ensuring predictable rhythm across dense product grids, checkout funnels, and marketing banners.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Low-contrast outlines** combined with subtle **Tonal layers** rather than heavy drop shadows. 

Cards and interactive containers use crisp 1px borders (`#D5D9D9`) against clean white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`). Hover states elevate elements slightly using soft, multi-layered ambient shadows to indicate interactivity without introducing visual clutter.

## Shapes

We employ a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness` level 1). UI elements feature subtle, restrained rounding (0.25rem for standard inputs and buttons, up to 0.5rem for major surface containers). This maintains a professional, utilitarian aesthetic while softening harsh geometric intersections in dense data lists.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#FF9900` background with dark text (`#0F1111`), 0.25rem border-radius, and a pronounced hover state darkening to `#F78A00`.
- **Secondary:** Clean white background with a 1px solid border (`#888C8C`), dark text, and a light gray hover fill (`#F7FAFA`).

### Input Fields
- High-contrast text inputs featuring a 1px neutral border (`#8D9494`), expanding to a primary-colored focus ring (`#FF9900`) with a subtle 2px glow.

### Cards
- Product cards utilize pure white surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px border, incorporating padded interior zones for imagery, metadata, star ratings, and distinct pricing blocks.

### Badges & Chips
- Promotional badges use solid high-contrast fills like `#CC0C39` for discounts or `#232F3E` for prime eligibility, paired with bold white small-caps text.
